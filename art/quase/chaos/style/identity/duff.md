## Mandatory likeness QA gate (owner correction, 2026-11-26)

Before showing any new Duff artwork as a candidate final, compare it with the most recent owner-approved model and tattoo references, not an earlier generated draft. If a reference is missing, ask for it; do not invent hidden motifs or assume the older art is accurate.

- **Anatomy and pose:** trace each arm continuously from shoulder to elbow, wrist and hand. Verify the character's own left and right against the front/back or profile reference; check thumb side relative to the palm, finger order and five digits where visible, wrist rotation, gripping pose and joint continuity. A duplicated/mirrored right hand, extra digits or impossible wrist fails. Use a neutral anatomically clear pose if the desired pose cannot be verified.
- **Tattoo surface:** distinguish palm-facing inner right forearm from dorsal/outer forearm and upper arm. Only the inner forearm has the seven-eyed wolf. A pose showing the outer surface must not display the wolf there; partial visibility is fine when anatomically appropriate. Verify all seven eyes if the whole tattoo is visible. Do not add invented art to the outer surface or copy a missing design from another limb.
- **Hands:** right back of hand = ornate dark script; left back of hand = central eye and geometric sunburst/mandala continuing into finger markings. Match the correct surface and side; do not invent legible script or move motifs to the palm.
- **Jewelry:** count four physically attached alternating gold/black cartilage hoops per ear where visible; count one gold-rimmed black gauge seated in each earlobe. Reject floating or duplicated jewelry.
- **Identity:** no blue rose, no mirrored tattoos, no invented tattoos. Compare crop at enlarged size, then check the entire image at thumbnail size. Record observed evidence or mark detail not visible. A failed or uncertain check blocks final/publication status; repair the smallest affected area and recheck, rather than declaring a pass from the prompt alone. Ask Duff before publishing a likeness final.

This gate applies to promo covers, character concept art, sprites, model turnarounds and textures. The owner's more recent actual references supersede older art. Keep personal reference photos private unless he explicitly approves publication.
